🎬 Weekly Movie

For this week's movie pick, I have chosen Knives Out directed by Rian Johnson.

Movie poster

Let's face it, good detective stories are sooo cool. These stories usually end with a climax: the final eureka moment when the detective brilliantly solves the intricate puzzle. How satisfying is that?? 🕵🏻

In this case, the brilliant detective is Benoit Blanc, played by Daniel Craig.

Knives Out was released in 2019 and it is a classic whodunit.

Here's a short summary of the plot (no spoiler).
Benoit Blanc is a famous private detective. He is hired to investigate on the mysterious death of Harlan Thrombey. Harlan is an old, wealthy novelist with a complicated relationship with his family. The person he trusts most is his personal nurse and friend Marta (Ana de Armas).
At first glance, the police establishes suicide as the cause of death. However, considering the large fortune left by Harlan, Blanc starts to suspect someone from the Thrombey family might have played a role in the mysterious death.
In fact, the reading of Harlan's will will shock the entire family...

The movie works on many different level.

- A complex scheme with a perfect solution. We are presented a complicated intrigue of events. However, with the final revelation, every piece of the puzzle, which may have seemed complicated at the beginning, ultimately fits perfectly into the frame.

- The role of the characters. Side-characters are often forgotten in whodunit movies. This is really not the case. Every member of the Thrombey family is unique and memorable and they all play an active role in the movie. My thumbs up for Jamie Lee Curtis 👍🏻 Harlan's daughter.

Linda Thrombey (Jamie Lee Curtis) about to discover a sad truth.

- A glance on the american upper class. This movie offers an interesting look at American society, especially upper class. This is reflected in the relationship between the Thrombey family and Marta (Harlan's nurse). Marta's family are immigrants. However, we never learn her true origin as each member of the Thrombey family assumes she comes from a different South American country.

- An almost touching finale. The end is a sort of David against Goliath finale. With Rolling Stones' Sweet Virginia in the background. Very Sweet.

My house, my rules, my coffee!!

Okay, a theme of the movie Knives Out is the relation between American society and South American immigrants... It was hard to find a song pick that might fit... 🥁

🎵 Weekly song

Don't You Worry 'Bout A Thing by Stevie Wonder

🎧 Listen Don't You Worry 'Bout A Thing on Spotify  •  ▶️ YouTube

Don't You Worry 'Bout A Thing is a song meshing latino vibes and american black music. It's from the album Innervisions, released in 1973.

You can hear the latino vibes already from the begging with a fantastic piano intro. The song hits more jazzy and bluesy notes in the chorus, with lots of chromatic scale, like the one at minute 1:38. Extremely difficult to sing 😱

Stevie was only 23 years old when he composed this album and had already released several masterpieces. Can you believe this guy??

Also the album cover is quite cool, with Stevie activating his innervision, or Inneres Auge, as someone might say 😇

I hope you will enjoy the listening 🙃
