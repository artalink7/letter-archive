Some stories are deeply rooted in a specific geographical area. Some places echoe the vibrations of the people who walked that ground.
That is the case of Seattle and Grunge.

The Battle of Seattle

This week story is set in the decade going from mid 80s to mid 90s, and takes place in the metropolitan area of Seattle.

Small aside. Many successful west-side stories started out of a garage: Bille Hewlett and David Packard designed the first HP product in a rented garage in Palo Alto; Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak moved Apple first headquarter in Job's parents home and they hand-built the Apple I in the family garage.

In this case, the garage is located in Bainbridge island, just in front of Seattle, and it belongs to the Wood's family. The two brothers Kevin and Andrew, use the garage to reharse their songs. The band is called Malfunkshun.

Mulfunkshun: Andrew and Kevin Wood, Regan Hagar.

I know what you're thinking: who the hell are Malfunkshun?
They became famous around Seattle at the end of the eighties, opening for Soundgarden, who were already quite famous. The band is a mix of glam rock and some sort of "speed" metal, so to say. Their sound is good, but the real star here is the singer: Andrew Wood.

Concert flyer, Mulfunkshun opening for Soundgarden.

Alhough Mulfunkshun never gained as much success as other bands from the same years, Andrew was recognized as the top performer around the city. Not only that, he was the real catalyst of the grunge subculture.

Kurt Cobain tells a funny story about Andrew Wood. He refers to him as "Andrew the Love God".

The Central Saloon is the venue for emerging bands in Seattle and it was probably here where Andrew met Jeff Ament and Stone Gossard, the bass and guitar players who will (a couple of years after) form Pearl Jam.

"The central location."

The three of them set up another band, called Mother Love Bone. However, Andrew never saw their first album printed out, as he died on March 16 1990, due to a heroin overdose...

The Grunge will go on

After the tragic death of Andrew Wood, Stone and Jeff decided to continue their career in music. They needed a singer, and it was not easy to replace Andrew... Somehow, they came to know about a former gas station attendant who had already covered the Route 66 a couple of times, following his (step) parents.
The ID of this Mr. Nobody says:

Name: Edward Louis.
Last Name: Severson.
Distinguishing marks: absolutely nothing. Actually, there is one, he is a hell of a singer.
Pseudonym: Eddie Vedder.

The Pearl Jam were born.

From 1990 to 1994, Pearl Jam shared the stage with their historic rivals: Nirvana (who don't need any kind of introduction). Both of the bands were led by two charismatic singers. Eddie on one side and Kurt on the other side.

The absolute peak was reached between August and September 1991. On August 27 Pearl Jam published Ten...

Ten - Pearl Jam. Album cover.

... and on September 24 Nirvana answered back with Nevermind

Nevermind - Nirvana. Album cover.

The grunge subculture will naturally spread in north america and eventually worldwide.
In 1992, the New York Times investigated on it: a reporter called a music label representative and he was told about a special "lexicon of grunge". The Grunge Speak was a total prank, just invented by the representative, a hoax, but the Times beleved it.

The Grunge speak words, as reported by New York Times in 1992. None of them were really used by anyone.

For writing this letter I did not swing on the flippity-flop and basically bound and hagged, but I guarantee I am no cob nobbler! 😬

Pearl Jam are still active and touring worldwide, while, as we all know, Nirvana ended when Kurt Cobain decided to end his life the Lincoln way (with a hole in the head... 💀).

🎬 Weekly Movie

For this week's movie pick, I have chosen Into the Wild directed by Sean Penn.

Into the wild - release poster.

Released in 2007, Into the Wild tells the real story of Christopher McCandless, a man who decided to abandon his old life and live in total isolation in the Alaska wilderness.

The reason I picked this movie is mainly due to the incredible soundtrack composed by the great Eddie Vedder! There is a high chance you have listened to some of the songs, even if you have not watched the movie.

Into the Wild is basically a road movie. Along his route to Alaska, Christopher (with nickname Alexander Supertramp), will meet curious and intriguing people from which he will learn how to adapt to his new, adventurous life.

The final message might seem to contraddict the actions of Christopher, as it reveal a simple truth:

"Happiness is only real when shared"
