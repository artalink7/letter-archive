For this week's movie pick, I have chosen Ghost in the Shell directed by Mamoru Oshii.

Motoko Kusanagi, the protagonist of the movie.

If you have ever read a book that has been originally written in Japanese, there's a high chance one of the first introductory pages was about the Hepburn system.
The Hepburn system is used for translating a japanese text into a language that uses a latin-based alphabet. The goal is to allow a non-japan reader to approximate the original phonology of the japanese words.

An italian explanation of the hepburn system.

The main character of Ghost in the Shell is major Motoko Kusanagi.

How should we pronounce the word Kusanagi, you may ask. That's a legitimate question... For example, how the 'g' should sound? Should it sound soft as in magic or hard, as in begin? Well, the Hepburn system tells us that the g is always pronounced as in begin.

Released in 1995, Ghost in the Shell is a japanese animated sci-fi movie.
It is set in a future time where humans have integrated cybernetic body parts. Some individuals have completely replaced their biological body with a full cybernethic prostethic body. This is the case of Kusanagi, a special agent investigating on cyber attacks.
The only trace left of Kusanagi's humanity is what in the movie is called ghost: an unidentified spirit that houses in the cyber body.

A prostethic skull.

Curiously, in the original screenplay, they use a transliteration of the english word "ghost" to describe the spirit. The actual word pronounced in the movie is gōsuto (using the hepburn method to write it).
Why did they avoid the use of an original japanese word? 🤔

Probably the author wanted to avoid any reference to religion. Most of japanese words that can be translated with "spirit" (or "soul") have profound connection to Shintoism.

An interesting one is Kami.

Three important Kami in Shintoism: Izanami and Izanagi on the left, Amaterasu on the right. If you read/watched Naruto, these names will ring a bell 🔔

In the Shinto tradition, Kami are spirits manifesting through objects as well as living things. They might represent deities, forces of nature or general qualities of beings. For example Amaterasu is the sun spirit/goddes, Omoikane is the spirit of wisdom.

Unlike the concept of "spirits" and "souls" in western cultures, the presence of a Kami is not necessarily associeted with living beings.

Might the ghost in the shell be a special kami?

The movie does not answer the question. In fact, it ultimetely lead to an open interpretation about the true nature of the ghosts in the shells.
